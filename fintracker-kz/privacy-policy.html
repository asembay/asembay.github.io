Privacy Policy FinTracker KZ respects your privacy. The app is designed as a local-first personal finance tool. Bank statements, transactions, categories, analytics, credit calculations and exported reports are processed on the user’s device. We do not collect, sell, share, upload or store your financial data on external servers. Data processed locally: • Imported bank statements • Transaction dates, descriptions and amounts • Categories • Analytics and summaries • User-created settings Network usage: The app may connect to public sources to show exchange rates. Your bank statements, transactions, balances and personal financial data are not sent with these requests. Data export: If the user exports data to CSV, the file is created locally and shared only by the user’s own action.
