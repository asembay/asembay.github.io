Frequently asked questions

Why did coins disappear after a round?

During play, the counter includes coins earned in the current attempt. They are added to your saved balance only when the entire round is completed without mistakes. If any answer was wrong, that attempt’s coins are not awarded. Your previous saved balance remains.

Why is the next round locked?

Complete the previous round and answer every question without mistakes to unlock the next island. You can retry a round after an unsuccessful attempt.

When do hints appear?

Tables 0 and 1 have their own illustrated explanations. In other rounds, a hint for the problem appears after two incorrect answers.

Why does the screen rotate?

On iPhone, menus and settings use portrait orientation; the round map and gameplay use landscape. On iPad, the interface uses landscape orientation.

How can I start over?

Open Settings → Clear game history, confirm the parent PIN, and confirm the reset. Rounds, the current attempt, answers, and coins are reset. Your PIN, settings, and parent-created rewards remain.

How do I change the language or turn off music?

Choose Russian, English, or Kazakh in Settings. Music, sound effects, decorative motion, and gentle button feedback can be turned off separately there.

How can I keep answer history?

Open history in the parent area and export it to CSV. You choose where to save the file and whom to share it with.

Can I move progress to another device?

The game has no server account or cross-device sync. Restoring a system backup depends on your Apple settings. Save any history you need before removing the app; support does not hold a copy of your progress.

What if I forgot my PIN?

The PIN is protected in Keychain. We cannot see it or recover it remotely. Contact support to discuss your options, and do not send the code itself.

How do subscriptions and free rounds work?

Tables 0 and 1 are free. The remaining tables and finals require active access. Apple offers eligible users 7 days free, followed by a monthly subscription. Adults can subscribe, restore purchases, and manage the subscription in the parent area. Progress and coins remain when a subscription ends.
