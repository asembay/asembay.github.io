1. An educational game

MathAlem For Kids helps children practise multiplication tables from 0 to 12. It is an additional learning tool; results depend on practice and do not guarantee a school grade. Parents can help and choose a comfortable pace.

2. Rounds and coins

The next round unlocks after completing the previous round without mistakes. During a round, the counter displays provisional coins. If an answer is wrong, that attempt’s coins are not credited to your saved balance; you can try again.

Coins are virtual game units. They are not money, cannot be exchanged for currency, and cannot be transferred between users.

3. Rewards created by parents

Parents determine the content, game-coin cost, approval, and delivery of family rewards. The developer does not sell or deliver these rewards. Adults are responsible for their safety, suitability for the child’s age, and fulfilling agreements with the child.

4. Your device and data

Keep your parent PIN secure. Do not add other people’s photos or materials without the necessary permission. Export, reset, and app removal are user-directed actions.

Progress is stored locally. Before removing the app or changing devices, check any backups you need and save your history. Data handling is explained in the Privacy Policy.

5. Rights to the app

The app’s code, design, characters, and other materials are protected by applicable rights. Using the app does not transfer those rights. Use and distribution through the App Store are also governed by applicable Apple terms.

The background melody “Meadow Footsteps” and sound effects were created specifically for MathAlem For Kids with AI assistance and programmatic sound synthesis. No third-party recordings or sample libraries were used. The music and sound effects belong to the author of the app.

6. Updates and support

Features and rules may be refined in updates. Contact support about bugs, accessibility, and use of the game. These terms do not limit user rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

7. Subscription and free trial

Tables 0 and 1 are free. Tables 2–12 and all finals require an active trial or monthly subscription. The learning sequence for unlocking islands remains unchanged.

Eligible users receive 7 days free after confirming the subscription through the App Store. The subscription then automatically renews with monthly billing unless canceled. The US base price is USD 3.99 per month; your region’s actual price, currency, and terms appear in the app and Apple’s purchase sheet before subscribing.

Payment is through your Apple Account. Manage the subscription or cancel renewal in Apple Account settings or through the app’s parent area. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Turning off renewal does not end access before the active period expires.

When access expires without renewal, tables 0 and 1 remain available. Coins, results, rewards, and checkpoints stay on the device, and play can continue after resubscribing. Restoring purchases restores access for the relevant Apple Account but does not itself transfer local game progress between devices. Apple handles refunds under its policies.
