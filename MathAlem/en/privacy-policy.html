MathAlem For Kids · Privacy

Your data stays yours

This Privacy Policy covers the current version of MathAlem For Kids, its support website, and email support.

The game requires no account and uses no ads or third-party analytics. Game data is processed on your device and is not automatically sent to the developer’s server.

1. Data on your device

The game saves your selected character and its name, language and preferences, completed rounds, checkpoints, answers, mistakes, answer timing, and coin balance. The parent area also saves rewards, descriptions, selected images, requests, and delivery history.

These records let you continue playing and review your child’s progress. They are stored locally in iOS; there is no mandatory registration or server profile.

2. Parent PIN

A PIN protects parent functions. A protected value derived from the code, rather than the plain-text code, is stored in the device’s Keychain. The PIN secret is not included in game files or CSV exports and is not synced by the app to a server.

PIN data is stored for this device only. Removing the app does not itself guarantee that iOS removes its Keychain entries.

3. Reward photos

A parent can choose a reward image using Apple’s system photo picker. The app receives the selected image, resizes it, and stores a local copy. This feature does not require access to the entire photo library; the image is not automatically sent to the developer.

You can remove the image from the reward without deleting the original photo from your library. The game does not use location, contacts, the camera, or the microphone.

4. Backups and export

Game files may be included in a system device backup, including iCloud, depending on your Apple settings. The game has no developer-operated cloud server or cross-device progress sync.

History is exported to CSV only when a parent requests it. You choose the destination and recipient; copies outside the app must be managed separately.

5. Children and parental control

The game is designed to help children learn multiplication with parental involvement. Children do not need to enter an email address, phone number, address, date of birth, or create an account.

The current version contains no ads or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Parent reward management and sensitive parent actions are PIN-protected. An adult should contact us with questions about a child’s data.

6. Contacting support

If you choose to email us, support receives your sender address, message, and any files you attach. These are used to reply and investigate the problem, not for advertising. The email service also processes the message under its own policies.

Correspondence is kept for the time needed to resolve the issue and related requests. You can request deletion of information you sent using the contact address below. Do not send PINs, passwords, payment details, or unnecessary information about children.

7. Apple and this website

The App Store, system backups, photo picker, and iOS diagnostic settings are governed by Apple’s relevant policies. Opening the review page opens an Apple service, which processes review information.

These website pages contain no data collection forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or external fonts. When you visit, GitHub Pages hosting may process technical information such as your IP address and request information under GitHub’s policy.

8. Managing and deleting data

In the parent area, you can edit names, rewards, and images, and export or clear history. Clear game history in Settings resets rounds, the current attempt, answers, and coins; it keeps your PIN, preferences, and parent-created rewards.

Removing the app using iOS deletes its local game files. Manage system backups, exported files, and Keychain entries separately; their retention may differ. The developer has no remote access to your local progress.

9. Updates and contact

This policy will be updated if features or data handling change. The current date is shown at the top of the page. Contact support with privacy questions or requests to delete information from correspondence.
