For help with scanning, imports, text recognition, PDF export, or subscriptions, email yekaterina.kim90@gmail.com.

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Do not attach confidential documents unless you choose to share them.

Subscriptions and purchases

To restore a purchase, open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. To manage or cancel an active subscription, use Manage Subscription in the app or your Apple Account subscription settings.

We cannot access documents stored inside the app on your device.
